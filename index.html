Yoo Rabbiin jedhe, mana barumsaa amantii fi saayinsii sadarkaa olaanaa qabu hundeessuun dhaloota beekumsaan, naamusaan fi sodaa Rabbiitiin cime horachuu.

Ergama (Mission)

  • Da'awaa qulqulluu babal'isa.
  • Tokkummaa Muslimootaa cimsuuf hojjeta.
  • Hojii kheeyrii fi tajaajila hawaasaa ni babal'isa.
  • Dargaggoota hoggansa gaarii qaban ni guddisa.

About Jam'aa Subul-Assalaam

Dargaggoota Waaccuu — United in Faith, Knowledge, and Service

Jam'aa Subul-Assalaam Dargaggoota Waaccuu is a youth association established in 2024 G.c in Ciiroo, Waaccuu Zone, West Hararge, Oromiya. We are committed to teaching the Qur'an and Sunnah, spreading pure Da'awaa, strengthening Muslim unity, and serving the community.


Core Values: Ikhlaasa, Amanamummaa, Haqaqabeessummaa, Marii (Shuuraa), Tokkummaa, Wal kabajuu, Itti gaafatamummaa, Hojii qulqullina qabu.
