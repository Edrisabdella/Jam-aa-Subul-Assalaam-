🎯 Mul'ata (Vision)

Yoo Rabbiin jedhe, mana barumsaa amantii fi saayinsii sadarkaa olaanaa qabu hundeessuun dhaloota beekumsaan, naamusaan fi sodaa Rabbiitiin cime horachuu.

Qur'aanaa fi Sunnah barsiisuu, Da'awaa qulqulluu babal'isuu, Tokkummaa Muslimootaa cimsuuf hojjechuu, Hojii kheeyrii fi tajaajila hawaasaa babal'isuu, Dargaggoota hoggansa gaarii qaban guddisuu.

About Jam'aa Subul-Assalaam

Dargaggoota Waaccuu — Youth Association

📖 Who We Are

Jam'aa Subul-Assalaam Dargaggoota Waaccuu is a youth association established in 2024 G.C in Ciiroo, Waaccuu Zone, West Hararge, Oromiya. We are committed to Islamic education, community service, and youth empowerment.

Bara Hundeeffamaa: 2024 G.c

Teessoo: Ciiroo, Zoonii Waaccuu, Godina Harargee Lixaa, Naannoo Oromiya

⭐ Core Values

  • ✅ Ikhlaasa (Sincerity)
  • ✅ Amanamummaa (Trustworthiness)
  • ✅ Haqaqabeessummaa (Transparency)
  • ✅ Marii (Consultation)
  • ✅ Tokkummaa (Unity)
  • ✅ Wal kabajuu (Mutual Respect)
  • ✅ Itti gaafatamummaa (Accountability)

📜 Kaayyoo fi Galma (Objectives & Goals)

Kaayyoo Waliigalaa
  • Qur'aanaa fi Sunnah barsiisuu
  • Dargaggoota amantii isaanii irratti cimsuu
  • Da'awaa babal'isuu
  • Hawaasa tajaajiluu
  • Tokkummaa Muslimootaa cimsuu
  • Madrasaa fi dhaabbilee barnootaa hundeessuuf hojjechuu
Galma (Specific Goals)
  • Madarasaa hundeessuu
  • Mana kitaabaa Islaamaa banuu
  • Leenjii fi muhaadaraa qopheessuu
  • Hawaasa dadhabaa gargaaruu
  • Qabeenya waaraa uumuun of danda'uu
